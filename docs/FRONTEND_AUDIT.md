## Deliberately NOT fixed — needs a team decision

**White-on-coral on legacy `.primary` buttons: 2.67:1** across Day, Night and
Night-vision, on `/record`, `/log`, `/declaration`.

This is the *documented* tradeoff already recorded in `CLAUDE.md`:

> `--action-contrast: #2A0C06` … white-on-coral measures 2.81:1 and fails …
> *(styles.css still paints legacy buttons white-on-coral — pre-existing, left
> untouched by the render-identically rule.)*

New components already use dark-on-coral and pass. Fixing the legacy sheet
means changing the most prominent button on every shipped page — a redesign
decision, not a drive-by. **This is the single highest-impact contrast fix
available.**

Similarly left alone: `p.banner` (the permanent AI-limitation disclaimer) at
14 px app-wide, and `p.bento-sub` at **13 px** — off the 12/14/16 scale
entirely. Both are type-scale changes that would shift layout on teammates'
in-flight pages.

## Systemic: the 48 px vs 56 px touch floor

`CLAUDE.md` mandates **56 × 56 px**. `styles.css` sets `button, a { min-height: 48px }`
app-wide, so essentially every legacy control is 48 px:

- `button.lk-header-btn` 48 × 48 (theme + a11y toggles)
- `button.lang-switch` 64 × 48
- side-nav links 220 × 48
- `.primary` / `.secondary` buttons ~48–50 px tall

`--touch-min: 56px` exists in `tokens.css` and new components honour it; the
legacy sheet predates it. Raising one line moves every button in the app, so
it belongs in the redesign — but it should be **first** on that list, because
it's one declaration and it's the floor the whole product is justified on
(gloves, wet hands, a moving boat).
